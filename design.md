# AfriWear E-commerce - Design Style Guide

## Design Philosophy

### Core Concept: "African Heritage, Modern Elegance"
The design embodies the intersection of rich African textile traditions and contemporary fashion sensibility. We create a warm, sophisticated aesthetic that speaks to culturally proud consumers who want to celebrate their heritage through premium, beautifully crafted clothing.

### Color Palette: Warm Earth Tones
- **Primary**: Warm White (#FDF8F2) - Clean, inviting, reminiscent of natural linen
- **Secondary**: Oat (#F7EFE0) - Soft, neutral, organic warmth
- **Accent**: Fog Gray (#EDE5D8) - Sophisticated, understated depth
- **Text**: Charcoal (#2C2C2C) - Strong contrast, readable, grounded
- **Highlight**: Terracotta (#C0622A) - Bold, warm, inspired by African earth pigments and Ankara dyes

### Typography
- **Display Font**: Canela (serif) - Elegant, editorial, suited to luxury fashion
- **Body Font**: Suisse Int'l (sans-serif) - Clean, modern, highly readable at all sizes

### Visual Language
- **Textile Focus**: Emphasise the rich, tactile quality of hand-woven Kente, Ankara prints, and Aso-Oke through warm photography and layered visual depth
- **Minimalist Approach**: Clean layouts with generous white space to let the garments breathe and command attention
- **Cultural Elements**: Incorporate organic patterns, warm gradients, and earthy tones that echo African textile traditions
- **Modern Sophistication**: Balance cultural warmth with a premium, contemporary retail aesthetic

## Visual Effects & Styling

### Used Libraries & Effects
1. **Anime.js**: Smooth micro-interactions and element transitions
2. **ECharts.js**: Data visualisation for product and sales metrics in the admin dashboard
3. **Splide.js**: Product image carousels and collection sliders
4. **p5.js**: Organic background patterns inspired by African textile motifs
5. **Matter.js**: Subtle physics-based interactions for product displays
6. **Pixi.js**: Advanced visual effects for hero sections

### Animation & Interaction
- **Scroll Animations**: Gentle reveal effects with 16–24px vertical translation
- **Hover Effects**: Subtle lift on product cards, warm glow on buttons
- **Loading States**: Smooth spinner animations in brand terracotta
- **Transitions**: 300ms ease-out for all interactive elements

### Header & Navigation Effects
- **Background**: Warm white with subtle blur backdrop filter
- **Navigation**: Semi-transparent with smooth scroll-triggered shadow
- **Logo**: Canela serif wordmark — "AfriWear" — clean and confident

### Product Showcase
- **Card Design**: White backgrounds with soft shadows and 20px border radius
- **Image Treatment**: Square aspect-ratio product photography with natural, warm lighting
- **Hover States**: Gentle lift effect with terracotta overlay revealing a Quick View button
- **Size Selector**: Compact dropdown integrated into the card footer

### Background & Atmosphere
- **Primary Background**: Warm white-to-oat gradient across the full page
- **Section Differentiation**: Alternating oat and fog-gray panels for visual rhythm
- **Accent Areas**: Charcoal panels (newsletter section) for high contrast moments
- **Texture Elements**: Subtle hero background image at low opacity to add depth without distraction

### Interactive Elements
- **Buttons**: Rounded corners, terracotta primary, outlined secondary
- **Forms**: Clean inputs with terracotta focus borders
- **Filters**: Checkbox-based with custom terracotta checked state
- **Cart**: Floating circular cart button with animated badge counter

### Mobile Considerations
- **Touch Targets**: Minimum 44px for all interactive elements
- **Typography**: Fluid font sizes using `clamp()` maintaining hierarchy across breakpoints
- **Spacing**: Generous padding for comfortable mobile browsing
- **Navigation**: Hamburger-triggered slide-down drawer with all nav links and sign-in state
- **Filters**: Collapsible sidebar toggled by a dedicated "Filters" button on mobile
- **Admin Sidebar**: Off-canvas slide-in panel with overlay on screens below 900px

## Brand Personality
Culturally proud, warm, and premium. AfriWear should feel like stepping into a beautifully curated boutique that honours African craftsmanship — confident in its heritage, welcoming to all, and effortlessly stylish. The design celebrates the artisans and weavers behind every garment while delivering a seamless modern shopping experience.
