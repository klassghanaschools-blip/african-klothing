Where It Began

A Deep Respect for African Textile Traditions

My African Wear was born from a simple belief: that fashion is a living expression of identity. Our journey began with a deep respect for the rich textile traditions of Africa — from the hand-woven Kente of Ghana to the vibrant Ankara prints of West Africa and the regal Aso-Oke of Nigeria.

We saw a gap between the extraordinary craftsmanship of African artisans and the global stage they deserved. So we set out to bridge it — bringing authentic, beautifully made garments to people who want to wear their culture with pride.

The Makers

Skilled Artisans, Centuries of Craft

We work directly with skilled artisans and weavers across West Africa. These are craftspeople who have inherited their techniques through generations — families who have been weaving Kente since childhood, dyers who know the exact temperature to set an Ankara print, tailors who can cut an Agbada with their eyes closed.

Every piece in our collection is made by hand. That means no two garments are exactly alike, and every stitch carries the story of the person who made it. When you wear My African Wear, you wear their work, their heritage, and their pride.

The Fabrics

Six Traditions, One Collection

Each fabric we use carries centuries of cultural meaning. Here are the traditions at the heart of My African Wear.

Ghana

Kente

Hand-woven silk and cotton strips sewn together into bold geometric patterns. Each colour and pattern carries specific meaning in Ghanaian culture.

West Africa

Ankara

Vibrant wax-resist dyed cotton with bold, colourful prints. Ankara is the heartbeat of West African street fashion and ceremonial dress alike.

Nigeria

Aso-Oke

A hand-loomed Yoruba fabric woven in narrow strips and sewn together. Worn at weddings, naming ceremonies, and celebrations of life's milestones.

West Africa

Dashiki

A colourful garment covering the upper body, featuring intricate embroidery around the collar and chest. A symbol of African pride worldwide.

Senegal / Mali

Boubou

A flowing, wide-sleeved robe embroidered with intricate patterns. The grand Boubou is the pinnacle of West African formal dress for men.

Pan-African

Wrap Styles

From wrap dresses to skirt sets, Ankara wrap styles celebrate the female form with bold prints and silhouettes that honour African femininity.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Every decision we make — from the artisans we partner with to the fabrics we source — is guided by these four commitments.

🤝

Artisan First

We pay fair prices directly to the artisans who make our garments, cutting out middlemen and ensuring the people who do the work are the ones who benefit.

✨

Authentic Always

We never compromise on authenticity. Every fabric is sourced from its region of origin, and every technique is the real thing — not a mass-produced imitation.

🌎

Community Rooted

A portion of every sale goes back into the weaving communities we work with — funding apprenticeships, tools, and the preservation of traditional techniques.

🌟

Modern & Proud

We believe African fashion belongs everywhere — on the street, at the office, at the ceremony. Our designs honour tradition while fitting into contemporary life.

Our Mission

Fashion as a Living Expression of Identity

Whether you are dressing for a ceremony, a celebration, or everyday elegance — My African Wear offers authentic styles for men and women that make you feel connected to something greater than fashion.

We believe that when you wear a piece of your culture, you carry forward the stories of the people who wove it, dyed it, and stitched it. That is not just clothing. That is heritage.
